# Effects, damage types & hazards

## Effects, damage types & hazards (cross-cutting)

- **EMP** — no damage; DV15 Cybertech or 2 cyberware/electronics inoperable 1 min (Cyberlimbs hang loose); disables
  decks/drones; immune via Hardened/EMP-Coating, FBC per-component, ACPA (Core).
- **Radiation** — high-level = treated as Mild/Deadly On Fire in-zone; Radiation Suit/FBC immune (Core, p.181).
- **Falling** — fall `40 m`/Turn; on first leaving solid ground, one DV15 Athletics check to grab a nearby ledge
  (auto-pass with a Grapple Hand/Gun); impact = `2d6 per 10 m` (body armor soaks) + DV15 Athletics or Broken Leg;
  2 Cyberlegs negate ≤30 m; a catastrophic fall is simply lethal (no Death Save) (Core, p.181).
- **Drowning** — out of breath after BODY minutes, then BODY direct-HP each Turn; bypasses/doesn't ablate armor
  (Core, p.180).
- **Asphyxiation** — as Drowning once out of air; in vacuum also drains INT/REF/DEX each Turn (INT 0 = dead),
  reversed on getting air (Core, p.180).
- **Electrocution** — `6d6`, soaked by armor (not a bypass), repeats while in contact (Core, p.180).
- **Exposure** — 1d6 direct-HP/day; no natural healing; negated by environment gear (Core).
- **Starvation** — no RAW default [cfg: after 7 days without food, a Death Save each day, +1 cumulative]
  (Appetite Controller, *Must-Have Cyberware Deals*).
- **Poison** — Resist Torture/Drugs vs DV by tier (Mild 11 / Strong 13 / Deadly 15); Mild/Strong/Deadly
  `1d6/2d6/3d6` direct-HP; no ablation (Core, p.180).
- **Biotoxin** — Resist Torture/Drugs vs DV by the same tiers (Mild 11 / Strong 13 / Deadly 15); direct-HP
  (Deadly 3d6); no ablation; delivered by vial/ammo (Core, p.345).
- **Drug** — Resist Torture/Drugs vs DV; on a fail, the drug's condition/effect + possible addiction (not HP
  damage) (Core, p.356).
- **Acid** — ablates SP, no HP (Core).
- **Inhaled gas/aerosol** — blocked by Nasal Filters / Anti-Smog Mask / sealed environment (Core).
- **Grapple** — opposed DEX + Brawling; on a win, grab the target (or take one held item) (Core, p.177).
- **Choke** — BODY direct-to-HP (ignores & doesn't ablate armor); 3 successive rounds → Unconscious regardless of
  HP (Core, p.177).
- **Throw** — BODY direct-to-HP (ignores armor) + target left Prone; ends the grapple (Core, p.177).
- **Nanite swarm** — area hazard needing no NET; Resist or direct-HP; filters immune (*So You Missed GenCon*).
- **Noise-scaled hazard** — damage dice scale with crew loudness (*So You Missed GenCon*).
