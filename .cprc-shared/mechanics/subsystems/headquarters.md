- **Headquarters** (*No Place Like Home*) — HQ IP currency (= Group IP/mission, crew-owned); each Improvement (40
  IP, +40 to Upgrade) grants a benefit (Medbay heal-as-BODY+2 & Medtech crafting, Server Room a NET Architecture &

  Evidence-Wall/Lounge/Studio); Morale Boost = 10-step repeatable ladder (Lifestyle −50eb, monthly Humanity, +LUCK
