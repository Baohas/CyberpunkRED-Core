# ACPA (power armor)

- **ACPA (power armor)** (*Solo of Fortune 2045*) — worn suit with **own HP & SP** taking hits before the pilot
  (pilot armor un-ablated); destroyed at 0 HP, immune to wound thresholds; no weak points / immune to Aimed Shots;
  Crits → 5 direct HP to pilot (0 for FBC); melee/MA don't halve its SP; immune to grapple/throw/choke (vs BODY
  ≤14), EMP, fire, contact/inhaled hazards, blind/deafen; sealed environment + 100 m radar/sonar; uses pilot
  REF/DEX/MOVE ± suit + a Linear-Frame BODY (no HP change); can't dodge bullets unless suit MOVE +3 / qualifying
  REF/DEX / Combat Awareness 6; entry/exit an Action; pop-up/plug body cyberware unusable inside; >4 h/day −1, >8 h
  −2; build = Frame (BODY/HP/MOVE) + Shell (SP/HP/MOVE penalty) + 4 A/B tunings; ≤6 onboard weapons + 6 cyberware
  (prereqs ignored, all paired); Weaponstech repair, no jury-rig.
