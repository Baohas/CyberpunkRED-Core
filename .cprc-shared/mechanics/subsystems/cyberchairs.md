# Cyberchairs

- **Cyberchairs** (Interface RED Vol 1) — mobility platform: the chair's MOVE replaces the pilot's, all movement
  options available; hands-free via Interface Plug or one dedicated hand; attacks hit the pilot; MOVE-lowering
  Crits hit the chair; EMP/Program-immune; the Spider model has 4 paired Cyberlimb-option slots.
