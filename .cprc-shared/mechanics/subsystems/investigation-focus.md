# Investigation / Focus

- **Investigation / Focus** (*Did Someone Say Murder?*) — Focus pool spent by failing Evidence Checks/Obstacles,
  refreshed by rest (+INT+WILL, +5 on a DV15 Concentration); a mystery has **Complexity** (HP) reduced by
  Evidence-Check damage, minus the clue's **Obfuscation** (armor SP); 1 Evidence Check/clue/day; aimless DV15
  Deduction hint costs 1d6 Focus.
