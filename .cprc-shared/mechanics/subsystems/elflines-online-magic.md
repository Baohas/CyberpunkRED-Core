# Elflines Online — Magic

- **Elflines Online — Magic** (*ELO Magic Returns*) — spells cast from a **Spellstone** (primary; secondary enables
  Enhanced versions); casting pays an **HP Cost** (direct damage to the caster — HP *is* the ammo) then an ROF1
  `INT + Magic` Check vs Range-type DVs; shapes Single/Barrage/Storm/Breath/Wave; elementality Air/Earth/Fire/Water
  (Select Elementality = caster picks); no Aimed Shots unless stated; some spells corrupt/purify a Spellstone;
  effects can reduce/zero HP Cost; "once per combat" limits.
