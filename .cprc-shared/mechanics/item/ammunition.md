# Item: Ammunition

## Ammunition

- **Price** — set by **ammo-type Price Category** [cfg]: Basic & Rubber `10eb` (Cheap); Smoke & Teargas `50eb`
  (Costly); Armor-Piercing, Expansive, Flashbang, Incendiary & Poison `100eb` (Premium); Biotoxin, EMP, Sleep &
  Smart `500eb` (Expensive). Grenades & rockets are priced **per unit** and **Armor-Piercing is their basic
  level**; a weapon/ammo may override the category price [cfg: e.g. a proprietary 30-round brick at `20eb`]
  (Big Dreem, Black Chrome Plus, p.3) (Core, p.344).
- **Sale stack size** — `10` rounds per purchase; grenades & rockets sold **singly** [cfg: varies per ammo/weapon
  — e.g. `50` (Junk), `100` (Small Game), `30`-round bricks (Big Dreem), `18` (Nova 757 Cityhunter), packs of
  `12` (Doberman 500 Marking Scent), or single nets] (Junk Ammunition, Black Chrome Plus, p.3) (Core, p.344).
- **Power source** — most weapons consume rounds, but some Exotic/Tech weapons run a **rechargeable battery pack**
  (RAW: `8` shots, 1-hour recharge, spare pack `50eb` Costly) drained per shot instead of ammo — model as
  `{rounds | battery}` [cfg] (Microwaver, Core, p.348).
- **Gun-locked / proprietary ammo** — a weapon may fire **only** its own ammo type (still at the category price);
  a per-weapon compatibility lock [cfg] (Nova Model 757 Cityhunter, 12 Days of Gunmas, p.5).
- **Bundled ammo** — a weapon's price may **include ammo pre-loaded** [cfg] (GunMart Engage Rocket Launcher ships
  with one AP Rocket, Black Chrome, p.107).
- **Free / improvised ammo** — a weapon may use effectively free ammo at `0eb` [cfg] (Nomad Rocker fires rocks,
  Black Chrome, p.115).
- **Damage modifier** — ±Xd6 (Junk/Small-Game; which also lower the weapon's **Autofire Rating** by 1, min 3,
  *Black Chrome Plus*); 5s count as 6 for Crits; reroll/extra-Crit (Expansive); −2 attack (Explosive); evasion
  penalty (High Velocity); Autofire +1 (Tracer) (Core, p.256/345).
- **Armor interaction** — `ablate 1` default `[cfg: 2 (Armor-Piercing, Core p.345); 4 (proprietary); 0 (Rubber);
  marker round ablate-1-no-damage (Blue Period, *Seeing Double*)]`.
- **Status/effect ammo** — Resist (Torture/Drugs or Cybertech) → Crit / direct damage / condition (Poison,
  Biotoxin, Sleep, Teargas, Flashbang, EMP, Incendiary; ModBall analogues) (Core, p.345).
- **Area/zone** — Grenade/Rocket AoE (default AP, *Errata*); Smoke (obscure `10*10`, −4); CO2 (extinguish) (Core,
  p.346).
- **Delivery** — drug-injection munitions; planted explosives (Demolitions: arm delay, defuse) (Core, p.345).
- **Compatibility** — type per weapon; Smart needs a Smart weapon + Targeting Scope; Exotics can't load Non-Basic
  unless stated; Rubber counts as Basic for Exotics (*Errata*); combine two non-smart non-explosive types at −4
  (Experimental Load, *Invented Tech Upgrades*); reusable adapter casings convert one ammo type to another (box of
  10, `100eb`) (Bullet to Slug Adapter Casings, *Black Chrome Plus*, p.3) (Core, p.344).
- **Crafting/gating** — Tech-only by default; non-Tech with a Kit (ModBall); Maker-Rank-gated (Core).
