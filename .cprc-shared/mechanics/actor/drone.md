# Actor: Drone

## Drones

- **Stat model** — no STAT array, Humanity, LUCK or EMP, and no Death Saves: a Drone has HP (+SP for security),
  MOVE, a finite battery and loadout slots, plus a Combat Number for security drones, and is **Destroyed at 0 HP**;
  under Direct Control it acts on the **operator's** skills.
- **Drones** — two classes: industrial/security (need a NET Architecture, count as Active Defenses) and personal
  (link to an Agent, no NET); modes Standby / Auto (autonomous, verbal commands, can't attack/dodge) / Direct
  Control (an Action via the Interface **Control** ability; attacks/dodges use the operator's skills; **can't dodge
  ranged** regardless of REF); switching modes free; `6 m` Agent tether (CitiNet drones excepted); link/counter = 5
  min within 6 m; HP (+SP for security), MOVE, finite battery, loadout slots (*All About Drones*).
