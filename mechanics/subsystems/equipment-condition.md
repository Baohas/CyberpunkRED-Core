# Equipment Condition

- **Equipment Condition** (*Breaking Your Stuff*; optional) — adds a **Damaged** state; GM-discretion triggers
  (poor maintenance, area Crit, electrocution, 20+ single hit, fire/radiation, repeated toxins); Lifestyle-gated
  maintenance removes Damaged + clears malfunctions; malfunction = 1d6 per-gear-type table (only Damaged can
  malfunction); jury-rig; Damaged/Destroyed resell one/two tiers lower.
