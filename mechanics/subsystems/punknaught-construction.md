# Punknaught construction

- **Punknaught construction** (*The Dreaded Punknaught*) — crew of 10; Salvage→Assemble (time = sum of all parts'
  build time incl. failures)→Activate (Land Vehicle Tech vs DV 13 + sum of part modifiers; fail = parts Destroyed
  Beyond Repair); lifespan in hours
  = margin over the Activate DV; parts axis = defense/melee/ranged/ramming/mobility; Death-Saves vs builder's TECH
  → d6 catastrophe.
