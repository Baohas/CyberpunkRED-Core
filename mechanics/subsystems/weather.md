# Weather

- **Weather** (*Night City Weather*) — Temperature (Cold→Exposure, Hot→+1 Armor Penalty), Conditions (rain −1/−2),
  and Strange Weather (Acid/Blood Rain, Ash/Dust/Smog poison+Foreign-Object, Radioactive Windstorm, Deadly
  Thunderstorm lightning, Cold Snap/Heat Wave override Temperature, Flooding, Blackout); protective gear negates
  specific effects.
