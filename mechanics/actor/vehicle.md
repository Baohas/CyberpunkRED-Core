# Actor: Vehicle

## Vehicles

- **Stat model** — no STAT array, Humanity, LUCK or EMP and no Death Saves: a Vehicle's hit points are **SDP**
  (0 = Destroyed) and it acts on its **operator's** `REF + Control Skill`; it is also a purchasable item with a
  Price Category. Stats: SDP, SP, Seats, Combat Speed (MOVE) (Core, p.189).
- **Stats** — SDP (0 = Destroyed, also stops being cover); SP (none by default; Armored Chassis SP13); Seats;
  Combat Speed = MOVE (the translation of narrative MPH); Nomad Access; dual stat lines (land/air, surface/sub)
  [cfg: SDP, Seats and speed-upgrade eligibility may be **locked per chassis** — e.g. SDP 35, no Heavy Chassis
  (Makigai Ebi, *Black Chrome*)] (Core, p.189).
- **Onboard systems** — a vehicle may mount its own attacks (Flashbulb Beacon blinder, *Invented Tech Upgrades*)
  or **self-drive** via its SAAI Agent at Skill Base 10 [cfg] (*Invented Tech Upgrades*).
- **Control** — Control Skill (Athletics for bikes); Interface Plugs = hands-free; Basic Driving needs `REF +
  Control > 9` else a DV10 check/Turn; start = top of Initiative (Core, p.192).
- **Maneuvers** — `REF + Control + 1d10` vs DV (Swerve/Sharp Turn/Emergency Stop/Landing 13; Bootleg/Jump/
  Aerobatic 17; NOS/PIT/Pull); fail = Lose Control (Core, p.192).
- **Repair** — by damage tier: Minor 9 / Major 13 / Destroyed 17 (Core, p.140).
- **Ramming / Lose Control** — `6d6` (3d6 bikes) to both + Whiplash to occupants; a **Lose Control impact is
  resolved as a ram** (same damage/Whiplash); dodge a ram on foot at DV13; exceptions take no ram damage / no
  Whiplash (Punknaught) [cfg] (Core, p.192).
- **Weak points** — Aimed Shot `*2` through SP (auto-hit stationary, DV13 moving); anti-Aimed upgrades (Core, p.191).
- **Upgrades** — Nomad list; rooms; Submersible (skill swap); weapon mounts; per-vehicle incompatibilities;
  Armored-Chassis +SP/−Speed (MetalGear), Agent self-driving, Flashbulb blinder (*Invented Tech Upgrades*) (Core).
- **Chase subsystem** — square-grid (1 square = 1 range band); each round a **Positioning Check** (vehicle skill
  vs a DV by Combat Speed: 60→13, 40→15, 20→17, 15→21, 10→24, 8→29) moves +1 square; <½ SDP drops a speed tier, <8 MOVE = crash; Maneuvers NOS/PIT/Pull
  Ahead/Pull In Close/Ramming; ends at >8 squares; a non-vehicle can join via a Speed value + Athletics
  (*Hot Pursuit* / *So You Missed GenCon*).
- **Human-powered (bicycles)** — ridden with **Athletics** (not Drive), Basic-Tech repair, no Interface control,
  **own Initiative** (not top of queue), collision **3d6** (not 6d6) + rider Whiplash & Prone; **Riding Fatigue**
  = an Endurance check after a long ride or be Fatigued; skates/skateboard = +4 m on a Run (*Spinning Your
  Wheels*).
