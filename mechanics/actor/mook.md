# Actor: Mook / NPC

## STATs, HP & death

- **STATs** — NPCs are built on the **same ten-STAT array as Characters** (INT, REF, DEX, TECH, COOL, WILL, LUCK,
  MOVE, BODY, EMP), but it is normally **pre-summed into the Skill/Combat Number** below; published mooks rarely
  list raw STATs (Core, p.412).
- **HP / wounds / Death Saves** — same system as Characters: `HP = 10 + 5 * ((BODY + WILL) / 2, round up)`,
  Seriously Wounded at `½ max HP`, Death Save `= BODY`; GMs may instead drop Mook-tier NPCs the moment they hit
  Seriously Wounded for speed [cfg] (Core, p.79, p.412).

## NPC abstraction, tiers & encounters

- **NPC abstraction** — skills stored as a pre-summed **Skill/Combat Number** (STAT+Skill+mods baked in, +1d10);
  no Role/Role-Ability/LUCK unless noted; cyberware-modified stats shown `effective (alt)`; can't dodge bullets
  (Core, p.412).
- **Adversary tiers** — Mook / Lieutenant / Mini-Boss / Boss; encounter budget weights (Lieutenant = 2 PCs, etc.)
  [cfg] (Core, p.412).
- **Encounters** — `2d10` → 1–100 table filtered by zone Threat Rating (Corporate avoids >50, Combat avoids <50,
  Executive = no table but Corp response in `1d10` rounds); ambush = one free side + Awareness-vs-Stealth to detect
  [cfg] (Core, p.399, p.417).
