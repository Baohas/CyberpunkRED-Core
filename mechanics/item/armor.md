# Item: Armor

## Armor

- **SP** — per location (head/body), normally bought separately [cfg: value per type; some are a **single item
  covering both locations**, repaired together] (Bodyweight Suit, Core, p.350) (Core, p.96).
- **Stacking** — only the highest SP source per location counts (Core, p.96).
- **Ablation** — −1 to all sources in a location, only when damage penetrates to HP (*Errata*) (Core, p.186).
- **Armor Penalty** — worst single penalty to REF/DEX/MOVE, applied once; negated at high BODY/FBC (Core, p.96).
- **Self-repair** — worn armor normally needs a Tech [cfg: some self-repair — `1 SP`/day passive (Executive Armor,
  *Black Chrome*) or `1 SP`/hour (Esporma Suit), or are **non-repairable** and fall off at 0 SP (Scavenged Armor)];
  implanted armor (Skin Weave/Subdermal) recovers 1 SP/day; Trauma Response Nanomatrix repairs to full
  (*Black Chrome*) (Core, p.114).
- **Melee/MA interaction** — ignore half SP (round up) [cfg] (Core, p.175).
- **Capability flags** — beyond SP, armor may grant immunities (fire/radiation/inhaled gas + built-in air supply),
  carry built-in weapons or cybereye/cyberdeck options, or impose extra REF/DEX/MOVE penalties [cfg per item]
  (Fire Brand Bunker Gear, *Black Chrome*, p.29).
- **Non-SP layers** — shields (HP 10/15; interpose = can't dodge; can't go in a Popup), implanted armor (jettison/
  recall), ACPA (own HP/SP) — see damage pipeline.
