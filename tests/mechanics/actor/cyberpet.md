# Actor: Cyberpet

- **Stat model** — an animal **NPC stat block**, statted like a Mook (Skill/Combat Number; same HP/wound/death
  system); the pet has **no Humanity score of its own**, but cyberware installed on it still follows the normal
  install/HL rules (see [overview.md](overview.md)).
- **Cyberpets** (*Your New Best Friend*) — Fixer-bought; pay the pet's own Lifestyle; bed-slots by tier; obeys at
  Animal Handling Base ≥ 10 (neglect → flee/turn); NPC stat block that may borrow Role Abilities + innate specials;
  cyberware install needs a Medtech w/ Science (Biology) 4+ + a day's training per piece; one piece may be 0-slot
  upgraded; animal cyberware is species-locked.
