# Item: Drugs & chemicals

## Drugs & chemicals

- **Street drug** — `{primaryDuration, secondaryDV (9 / 15 / 17 by drug), cost}` + Primary Effect (bonus,
  sometimes an HL cost) +
  Secondary Effect: a **Resist Torture/Drugs vs DV** check; fail → downside and **addiction** (persistent penalty
  while not dosed until cured) (Core, p.356). Primary Effects reach beyond stat buffs [cfg]: temporary Humanity Loss
  **returned** when it ends and a WILL boost that does **not** raise HP (Prime Time), buying an extra NET Action for
  1 HUM (Sixgun), or modifying Initiative mid-combat (Timewarp) (*Hornet's Pharmacy*).
- **Additive compounds** — mixed into a Vial of Poison/Biotoxin to modify it, no stacking: delay onset 1 min / 1
  hour, `+2` Resist DV, or grant contact/surface delivery [cfg] (*Hornet's Pharmacy*).
- **Pharmaceutical (Medtech)** — Antibiotic, Rapidetox, Speedheal, Stim, Surge, Sedative, Veritas, Immunoblockers
  (Core).
- **Chemical weapon** — Resist DV + delivery (Mortalis, Mindfire, Terrifier, Red/White Lace) (Core); the **Combat
  Chemistry** subsystem adds gas/injection war-drugs (Anti-Cerebral, Deliriant, Rime…) blocked by filters and
  interacting with cyberware, delivered via Multihypo / Arrowhypo / Airburst munitions (*Solo of Fortune 2045*).
- **Alcohol/intoxication** — karmotrine units → escalating Resist DV → Drunk −4 / Buzzed −2; Cyberliver +6
  (*Mixing Drinks Changing Lives*).
- **Delivery** — Action; Airhypo (willing, or Melee to dose unwilling); auto-injector; ammo munitions; gas/aerosol
  (Core, p.351).
