# Item: Weapons

## Weapons — type & item axes

- **Categories** — Melee (DEX), Ranged (REF), Exotic (specialised; usually Average quality; no attachments/Non-Basic
  ammo unless stated); cyberweapons are implanted variants (Core, p.91).
- **Damage by class** — melee `{Light 1d6, Medium 2d6, Heavy 3d6, Very Heavy 4d6}`; ranged single-shot dice per
  class [cfg: per item] (Core, p.91).
- **ROF** — per class (melee 2 except Very Heavy 1; ranged per row) [cfg: per item; conditional ramp, e.g. 1→2 if
  fired the previous Round (Militech Perseus, *Black Chrome*)] (Core, p.93).
- **Skill used** — type-default, or off-type (Flamethrower→Heavy Weapons, Survivalist→Archery); **Exotics** may fire
  with Heavy Weapons / Archery / Pilot and several may load Non-Basic ammo — widening the base Exotic restriction
  (*Woodchipper's Garage* / *12 Days of Gunmas*).
- **Concealable** — by class; granted/blocked by attachments/quality/cyberware (Core, p.91).
- **Requirements** — BODY to wield unmounted (jam/penalty if under, e.g. 11+ for some Exotics); biometric key;
  lowered −3 by an upgrade (Counterweight System, *Invented Tech Upgrades*) (Core, p.347).
- **Quality** — Poor (jam on Crit-Fail, 1 Action to clear) / Standard / Excellent (+1); Exotics Average; cost
  shifts by tier [cfg: worse Crit-Fail effects — destroyed beyond repair / electrocute / self-Crit the wielder
  (Scarlet Blackbow, *Elflines Online*); or an **earned/conditional** quality, e.g. Excellent after N kills
  (Hello Cutie 1TruLuv, *Black Chrome Plus*)] (Core, p.342).
- **Magazine & reload** — Standard/Extended/Drum capacities; 1- vs 2-Action reload; **battery weapons** (RAW: `8`
  shots, 1-hour recharge, spare pack `50eb`; some drain a pack per shot *in addition to* a bullet — Arasaka
  Prototype Variable Assault Rifle, *Black Chrome* p.102); bows need no Reload; mixed/dual-load ammo; ammo
  purchasing/price/stack overrides live in [ammunition.md](ammunition.md) [cfg: per item] (Core, p.343).
- **Attachments** — `3 slots` on non-Exotic ranged; type-specific; pay per slot; some take 0 slots or fit Exotics;
  Rebuilds — Power (+5 Crit bonus damage, ricochet) / Smart (+1 attack, Improved Smart ammo) / Tech (charge →
  through-thin-cover shot ignoring half SP), one per weapon (2070s, *Edgerunners Mission Kit*); silencer; bayonet;
  under-barrel GL/shotgun (2 slots); Smartgun Link (2); scopes; combine two under-barrels / add Microwaver-Stun
  mode (*Invented Tech Upgrades*); no-slot Exotic-compatible attachments + Range-Table-Modification swaps the
  weapon's range band (*Solo of Fortune 2045*) (Core, p.342).
- **Damage-modifier flags** — fixed-damage-regardless-of-BODY; ignores armor below SP N (e.g. SP7 mono-blade
  *Black Chrome* p.92 / SP11 Rostović Kleaver / SP12 Deathwind railgun); direct-to-HP; sets On Fire; non-lethal;
  +crit-roll behaviours (see Critical Injuries); inflicts a Critical Injury instead of damage (Militech Growler →
  Brain Injury, *Street Stories*); grants a conditional non-stacking skill bonus when worn/wielded (+2 Wardrobe &
  Style, Superchrome line, *Black Chrome*); disposable / one-shot (no reload; extracting ammo destroys it,
  *Toggle's Temple*); self-destruct/overheat on over-firing (Bubba Buster warps after 3 bursts, *Black Chrome*);
  flagged **cannot be Tech-Upgraded** (SurvivalMaster, *Black Chrome Plus*) (Core + supplements).
