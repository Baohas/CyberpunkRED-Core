# Edgerunners / 2070s

- **Edgerunners / 2070s** (*Edgerunners Mission Kit*) — **Neuroport** replaces the Neural Link (bundles Neural
  Link + Holophone/Biomonitor/Virtu/HUD/shard slots; free at chargen; most cyberware now requires it);
  **Quickhacks** — a Netrunner jacks a target's Neuroport (range 50 m, contested Interface vs WILL) then spends
  NET Actions on DV-gated quickhacks (Overheat, Short Circuit, Puppet, System Reset…); **Weapon Rebuilds**
  (Power/Smart/Tech, one per weapon); **iconic cyberware** (David's Sandevistan = no-Action top-of-queue for 2d6
  HL per use; Mantis Blades / Monowire / Gorilla Arm; Berserk Implant; Ex-Disk; Projectile Launch System);
  **Humanity Gain** incidents (1d6/2d6/3d6) alongside Loss; **Immunoblockers** (restore 2d6 Humanity, risky DV21
  secondary); direct purchase at `2*` cost to bypass a Fixer.
