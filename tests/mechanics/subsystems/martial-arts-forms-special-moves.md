# Martial Arts Forms & Special Moves

- **Martial Arts Forms & Special Moves** (*Cyberfists of Fury*) — each Form a separate `*2` DEX skill granting 2
  Special Moves; a Special Move = {trigger, stat/skill gate, resolution target (vs Evasion / fixed DV / opposed
  check / another Special Move), action cost, effect}; stances are toggled lasting states; overrides — Gun Fu
  (Handgun-as-dodgeable-melee, spends ammo), Kendo "Cut the Bullet" (melee-defend a ranged shot), act-while-
  unconscious dodge, Special-Move-vs-Special-Move counter; multi-limb moves (*So You Missed GenCon*).
