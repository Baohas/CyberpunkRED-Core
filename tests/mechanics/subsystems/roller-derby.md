# Roller Derby

- **Roller Derby** (*Chasing the Rabbit*) — jams as combat; 5-per-squad roles (2 Jammer / 2 Blocker / 1 Rabbit),
  win a jam at 10; MOVE+1d10 Initiative, no Run, eject at 0 HP or 3rd Crit; position-restricted attacks (penalty
  box); Actions Bump/Guard/Skate-or-Die/Whip; blunt weapons + SP7 Padding only.
